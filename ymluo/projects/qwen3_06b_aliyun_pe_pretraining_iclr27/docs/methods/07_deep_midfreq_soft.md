# 07 — Deep middle-frequency attenuation

**Question.** Are the gains specific to fast pairs, or can slowing the middle
band also improve retrieval?

In the deepest third only:

$$
\phi_{\ell i}(p)=0.25p\omega_i,qquad 16\le i<32.
$$

F0–F15 and F32 onward remain native. This is a band-location control. It
supports a middle-band mechanism only if it beats native and the effect differs
from the high- and low-band conditions under identical compute.

Config: `configs/strategies/deep_midfreq_soft.json`.
