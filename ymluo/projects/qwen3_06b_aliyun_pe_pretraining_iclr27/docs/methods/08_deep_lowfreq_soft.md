# 08 — Deep low-frequency attenuation

In the deepest third, F40–F55 use 0.25× phase while all other pairs remain
native:

$$
\phi_{\ell i}(p)=0.25p\omega_i,qquad40\le i<56.
$$

These pairs rotate slowly and are plausible carriers of broad position. This
condition tests whether slowing them extends range or instead removes the
remaining stable long-distance position signal. Worse retrieval would be useful
negative evidence against indiscriminate low-frequency suppression.

Config: `configs/strategies/deep_lowfreq_soft.json`.
