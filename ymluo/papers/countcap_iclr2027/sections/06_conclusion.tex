\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The frozen Robust path retains 99.930\% of Full on complete LongBench,
100.333\% on formal 650-pair RULER, and 99.805\% pooled PPL over 12
native-128K cases, without reranking, learning, or dense fallback.
Native-MHA Fast/Robust attention reaches
1.27--6.37$\times$/1.08--4.12$\times$ at 8K--128K, and Robust steady decode
reaches 1.32/2.22/3.98$\times$ at 32/64/128K.  A 64-token cold request reaches
.81/1.31/2.15$\times$, so reuse is an explicit regime.  Exact K/V remains
resident and auxiliary indices add 7.47\%; new prompts rebuild the basis and
index, sparse prefill is outside scope, and 32K cold latency is below parity.
We keep MHA speed and GQA task-quality contracts separate.  One 128K RULER
cell drops from 1.0 to .8 over five examples, so the result supports aggregate
rather than cell-wise equivalence; we stop the frozen generalization claim at
128K.
