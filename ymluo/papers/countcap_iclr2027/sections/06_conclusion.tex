\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\method's 5.86\% QK-balanced Key index preserves complete LongBench, but a
corrected native-128K audit shows that selected-only attention is not a
universal consumer.  The 7.47\% Robust profile closes this omitted-Value
failure on 12 topic/seed cases without reranking, learning, dense fallback,
or additional exact-attention tokens.  An earlier 64K-gated ablation improves
an 80-example short-context screen, but the boundary is not theoretically
derived and is not part of the frozen method.  Under the frozen native
  MHA contract, complete Fast and Robust attention paths reach
  1.27--6.37$\times$ and 1.08--4.12$\times$ from 8K to 128K.  Robust steady
  whole-model decode reaches 1.32/2.22/3.98$\times$ at 32/64/128K, while a
  64-token cold request including index construction reaches
  .81/1.31/2.15$\times$.  Short requests may not amortize initialization;
  persistent reuse is therefore an explicit evaluation regime rather than an
  implicit assumption.
Full RULER, cross-model, H100, and complete same-path Robust LongBench
evaluation remain required before final submission.
