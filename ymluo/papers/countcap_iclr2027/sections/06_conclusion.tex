\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\method's 5.86\% QK-balanced Key index preserves complete LongBench, but a
corrected native-128K audit shows that selected-only attention is not a
universal consumer.  The 7.47\% Robust profile closes this omitted-Value
failure on 12 topic/seed cases without reranking, learning, dense fallback,
or additional exact-attention tokens.  A deterministic General profile uses
Fast through 64K and Robust beyond it; an 80-example same-path LongBench
screen removes the short-context Robust regression.  Under the frozen native
MHA contract, the selector-plus-exact-sparse-attention path reaches
1.22--6.23$\times$ from 8K to 128K with a 5.53\% realized index.  This path
excludes the GQA-specific Value-tail sketch and is not a whole-model decode
claim.  Short requests still may not amortize initialization.
Full RULER, cross-model, H100, and complete same-path General LongBench
evaluation remain required before final submission.
