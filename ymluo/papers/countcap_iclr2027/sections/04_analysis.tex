\section{Analysis}
\label{sec:analysis}

\paragraph{Exact geometry.}
Substituting \cref{eq:qk-svd,eq:qk-factors} gives
\begin{equation}
 AD^\top=I,\qquad
 A^\top\widetilde C_qA=D^\top C_kD=\Sigma,
 \label{eq:main-exact-balance}
\end{equation}
so the unquantized transform changes no QK score.  Moreover, among rank-$r$
bilinear approximations, truncating this SVD minimizes expected squared score
error:
\begin{equation}
 \min_{\operatorname{rank}(W)\le r}
 \mathbb E(q^\top k-q^\top Wk)^2=\sum_{j>r}\sigma_j^2.
 \label{eq:main-rank-optimal}
\end{equation}
Concurrent SAKI derives the same covariance-weighted rank-$r$ endpoint for
floating-point low-rank indexing \citep{zhang2026saki}; our method uses its
full-rank endpoint before mixed-bit quantization.  Proofs, regularization
conditions, and the paired-dependence residual are in \cref{app:proofs}.

\paragraph{Why Query-weighted allocation.}
For quantization error $e=k'-\widehat k'$, independence across the empirical
Cartesian Query--Key sample gives
\begin{equation}
 \mathbb E(q'^\top e)^2=\tr(C'_qC_e),
 \label{eq:main-qmse}
\end{equation}
The allocator minimizes the sum of its within-band blocks in
\cref{eq:band-qmse}; this equals the full score MSE when either moment is
block diagonal in the chosen bands.  Otherwise the omitted cross-band term is
explicitly bounded and audited in \cref{eq:cross-band-bound}.  Key-MSE
$\tr(C_e)$ is correct only for isotropic $C'_q$.  Under Query drift
$\Delta=C'_{q,\mathrm{dec}}-C'_{q,\mathrm{cal}}$,
\begin{equation}
 |\tr(\Delta C_e)|\le\|\Delta\|_{\op}\tr(C_e).
 \label{eq:main-drift}
\end{equation}
This is a stability diagnostic, not an unconditional held-out guarantee.
Finite-sample, subspace, allocation-regret, and fixed-rate conditional bounds
are provided in \cref{app:proofs}.

\paragraph{Ranking and output.}
Let exact and proxy errors be $\delta_i=s_i-\widehat s_i$ and
$R_\delta=\max_i\delta_i-\min_i\delta_i$.  Any exact top-$B$ token whose
boundary margin exceeds $R_\delta$ remains selected.  The appendix converts
centered score RMSE into preserved-core and omitted-mass certificates.  For
the realized selected set $S$, let
$\epsilon=\sum_{i\notin S}p_i$.  Exact recomputation on $S$ yields
\begin{equation}
 \|p-\widetilde p\|_1=2\epsilon,\qquad
 \|o-\widetilde o\|_2
 \le\epsilon\,\diam\{v_i\}.
 \label{eq:main-output-bound}
\end{equation}
Thus selector quality is governed by score margins and omitted probability
mass, not set recall alone.

\paragraph{What Robust corrects.}
Write exact selected and omitted conditional Value means as $\mu_S,\mu_T$,
their selected mass as $p$, and Robust estimates as
$\widehat p,\widehat\mu_T$.  Its exact error decomposition is
\begin{equation}
 \widehat o_\alpha-o=(\widehat p-p)(\mu_S-\mu_T)
 +(1-\widehat p)(\widehat\mu_T-\mu_T),
 \label{eq:tail-error-decomposition}
\end{equation}
hence
\begin{equation}
 \|\widehat o_\alpha-o\|_2\le
 |\widehat p-p|\|\mu_S-\mu_T\|_2
 +(1-\widehat p)\|\widehat\mu_T-\mu_T\|_2.
 \label{eq:tail-error-bound}
\end{equation}
For zero-mean tail-estimation error with variance $V$ and
$D=\|\mu_S-\mu_T\|_2^2$, a tail mixture weight $w_\alpha$ has risk
\begin{equation}
 \mathbb E\|\widehat o_\alpha-o\|_2^2
 =((1-p)-w_\alpha)^2D+w_\alpha^2V,
 \label{eq:tail-bias-variance}
\end{equation}
and improves over Fast exactly when
\begin{equation}
 0<w_\alpha<2(1-p)D/(D+V).
 \label{eq:tail-correction-benefit}
\end{equation}
Robust therefore addresses an omitted-Value failure that better Key ranking
alone cannot remove, while its fixed shrinkage still requires held-out tests.

\paragraph{Cost and crossover.}
Both profiles scan $\Theta(N)$ low-bit entries; exact attention is capped by
\cref{eq:budget-intro}.  Robust also scans 66 Value bits/token.  If $f_t,s_t$
are directly measured Full and QKSieve step costs and $I_{\rm net}$ is
unhidden construction, QKSieve breaks even after $G$ tokens iff
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{t=1}^{G}(f_t-s_t)>I_{\rm net}.
 \label{eq:main-break-even}
\end{equation}
For Full non-attention cost $t_{\rm base}$ and attention cost $t_{\rm attn}$,
the whole-model ceiling is
\begin{equation}
 \mathrm{speedup}\le(t_{\rm base}+t_{\rm attn})/t_{\rm base}.
 \label{eq:main-amdahl}
\end{equation}
We therefore report cold, warm, and persistent reuse separately.
