\begin{abstract}
Autoregressive decoding repeatedly scans the complete key--value (KV)
history.  Query-aware sparse attention can remove most exact attention only
when its selector is both cheaper than dense QK scoring and faithful to the
joint Query--Key geometry.  We present \method, a training-free,
GPU-resident exact-KV retrieval method.  Request-local Query and Key second
moments define biorthogonal coordinates that preserve every unquantized dot
product exactly and order dimensions by joint score energy.  A per-layer,
per-KV-head allocator assigns 0/1/2/4/8 bits to eight 16-D bands under a
240-bit token/head budget by minimizing a separable bandwise logit-MSE
surrogate.  The
packed index selects
$B(N)=\min\{N,1280,\max(256,\lceil.06N\rceil)\}$ positions, after which
original FP16 K/V receive exact sparse attention.  No learned router,
exact-QK reranking, task rule, or dense fallback is used.

The selected-only Fast profile adds 5.86\% to the FP16 K+V footprint.  The
frozen Robust profile adds a current-Query rank-16 INT4 Value-tail estimate,
for 7.47\% total auxiliary storage.  At 1\% active KV, QK balancing improves
exact top-$k$ recall from 43.36\% to 73.44\% and selected attention mass from
76.40\% to 83.48\% over an equal-rate FIER reproduction.  The complete
3,750-example Llama-3.1-8B LongBench reference evaluation retains 99.88\% of
Full-attention macro score.  On 12 native-128K topic/seed cases, Robust
retains 99.805\% pooled perplexity quality, versus 96.319\% for Fast.

Directly timed native-MHA attention, including selection and exact sparse
attention, reaches 1.08--4.12$\times$ for Robust from 8K to 128K on an
RTX~3090; steady whole-model decode reaches 1.32/2.22/3.98$\times$ at
32/64/128K.  Persistent-cache measurements separately expose cold-build and
reuse costs.  We derive score-preservation, rate-distortion, ranking, and
omitted-mass bounds and report complete-path measurements rather than latency
decomposition.
\end{abstract}
