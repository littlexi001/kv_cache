\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Long-context retrieval can fail even when the evidence semantics are fixed.
RoPE modulates the evidence score non-monotonically through relative phase, and
softmax converts score changes into attention-probability changes.  Local
answer utility is not determined by phase suppression or attention amount
alone: it jointly depends on attention probability, the centered Value write,
and the downstream answer-margin gradient.  No-op-matched interventions
reproduce this local score-to-margin relation at 8K, 16K, and 24K, while exact
replay and activation patching support its propagation through the residual
stream.

The same analysis prevents an over-simple method claim.  Query-specific phase
edits are functionally logit edits, and one reusable GQA cache can realize only
separable Query/Key phase corrections satisfying the four-cycle condition.
Thus the paper's contribution is a mechanism and its identifiability boundary,
not a mature replacement for RoPE.  Full mediation estimates, a second model,
native-window full-chain activation-patching analyses, and natural retrieval
tasks remain necessary before the explanation can be called
architecture-general.
