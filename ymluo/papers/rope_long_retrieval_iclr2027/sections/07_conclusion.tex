\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

RoPE does not impose a simple monotone penalty on remote evidence.  It makes a
fixed content match a mixture of distance-dependent phases; unfavorable
interference can reduce its QK priority before softmax.  The changed attention
write then alters later pre-RoPE representations and can cross the output
decision boundary.  Exact first-layer reconstruction, layerwise finite-
difference replay, and activation patching jointly support this mechanism in
Qwen3-8B.  The same diagnosis yields a conservative repair: preserve local
RoPE and exact post-RoPE consumption, but add a position-free semantic proposal
for remote candidates.  \methodpost improves held-out controlled two-hop
retrieval most clearly at 16K--32K.  Establishing generality now requires
natural benchmarks, additional architectures, local-order controls, and an
efficient retrieval index; these are explicit boundaries of the current
claim.
