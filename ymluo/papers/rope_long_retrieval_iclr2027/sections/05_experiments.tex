\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We ask four questions.  Does the frequency-pair formula reconstruct a real
model's QK score?  Does the resulting state divergence affect the output?  Can
attention amount or phase suppression identify useful evidence?  Finally, can
the directed derivative in \cref{eq:directed-utility} predict a measured local
intervention across context lengths?

\subsection{Protocol}

\paragraph{Analytic phase reconstruction.}
We load Qwen3-8B in NF4 with BF16 compute and YaRN factor 2.  One seeded English
two-hop case supplies a gold token near the prefix and the terminal Query token.
We save their layer-0 pre-RoPE Q/K once, reuse those identical vectors at four
relative distances, and compare the explicit rotation with the 64-pair formula.
No answer-level conclusion is inferred from this single analytic case.

\paragraph{Extended-position trajectory.}
A separate, unquantized BF16 Qwen3-8B run uses SDPA and YaRN factor 4 on the
one-hop age-distractor protocol in \cref{sec:setup}.  Both cases derive from a
fixed 144K body stream whose full version contains $4{,}607$ wrong-age records
and period filler.  The shorter case uses its first $143{,}401$ body tokens;
the longer inserts the next 64 before the identical Query text: 47 periods and
17 distractor-record tokens, including 2 age tokens.  We save query-row Q/K, attention and MLP outputs,
residual states, and answer logits; then replay each BF16 update and patch one
residual input at a time.  This is one high-resolution, position-extended case,
not a population estimate or evidence about the checkpoint's native window.

\paragraph{Population singleton intervention.}
The main causal audit uses unquantized Qwen3-8B in BF16 eager mode at 8,192,
16,384, and 24,576 tokens on the source-conditioned single-hop age task defined
in \cref{sec:setup}.  Each length has 8 independent seeds.  Per seed, an
oracle ranks one coordinate from each of four classes---gold evidence, plausible
conflict, lexical/format distractor, and filler---by the absolute product of
positive grid-envelope suppression and $\partial m/\partial s$.  Each target is
paired with a random coordinate from the same layer, head, and class.  We add
$\epsilon=0.25$ to exactly one pre-softmax score and compare against the
identical $\epsilon=0$ path.  Replay, single-coordinate, prefix-cache, and
target/random-position audits must all pass.  The primary closure analysis uses
the 16 gold/conflict target events at each length.  Because decisive-token
identity is not matched, random coordinates are numerical controls rather than
a fair magnitude or signal-to-noise baseline.

\paragraph{Statistics and memory boundary.}
Confidence intervals use 50,000 percentile bootstrap samples with seed as the
cluster; layers, heads, and coordinates are not treated as independent
experimental units.  The 32,768-token BF16 extension produced no result: on
both 24GB RTX 3090 devices, native eager GQA \texttt{repeat\_kv} failed while
requesting an additional 256 MiB.  We report this as OOM rather than changing
the protocol after inspection.

The three protocols above support adjacent links under different controls; we
do not combine their measurements into a single same-example mediated fraction.

\subsection{Answer-directed score utility predicts local effects}

\Cref{tab:closure} compares the first-order prediction
$\epsilon U_{l,h,j}$ with the finite score intervention.  Agreement remains
high at all three lengths.  The slope stays near one and its intercept covers
zero in every run (full values in \cref{app:closure}).  ``Sign'' is the sign of
the measured margin change, not model answer accuracy.

Matched-random coordinates do not show a comparably stable relationship:
Pearson/Spearman are 0.582/$-0.065$ at 8K, with Pearson CI
[$-0.288$, 0.948]; 0.051/$-0.047$ at 16K; and $-0.226$/$-0.024$ at 24K.
However, target and random sets are not fully matched signal-to-noise controls:
the target selector is an answer-gradient oracle and the decisive-token fraction
differs.  The warranted conclusion is therefore local: the derivative closes
the chosen score-to-margin step, not that magnitude artifacts are excluded or
that the ranking rule is available at inference time.

\subsection{Answer-directed score utility reveals a mean gap}

We next average all baseline sampled layer--head--token rows within each seed
and class, then compare gold and conflict seed means.  Each seed/class contains
$2\times36\times32=2304$ rows: two sampled positions across 36 layers and 32
query heads.  These rows are not the 16 oracle intervention targets.
\Cref{tab:gold-conflict} reports four quantities.  ``Direct centered-OV'' is
$a_j(w_g-w_c)^\top W_O^{[h]}(v_j-\bar v_h)$ at the measured layer, where
$w_g,w_c$ are the gold and conflict rows of the LM-head unembedding.  It omits
all later normalization, residual, attention, and MLP Jacobians and is therefore
a local proxy, not a factor of the exact utility $U$.  ``RoPE suppression'' is the
grid-envelope gap
\begin{equation}
  \Gamma_j=\max_{d\in\mathcal D}s_j^{(d)}-s_j^{\mathrm{native}},
  \qquad
  \mathcal D=\{1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128\},
  \label{eq:grid-envelope-suppression}
\end{equation}
where $s_j^{(d)}$ reuses the same pre-RoPE Q/K content at anchor relative
distance $d$.  It is not simply a pre-minus-post score.  The exact downstream
utility and the local direct centered-OV proxy have positive gold-minus-conflict
mean gaps at all lengths with confidence intervals excluding zero.  The
attention-probability and grid-envelope-suppression intervals include zero.

The result answers a central ambiguity.  RoPE phase and softmax competition
govern how much a token enters the attention mixture.  They do not certify that
the token is true or useful.  Whether increasing its score helps depends on the
centered Value direction and its alignment with the downstream answer gradient.
We detect positive directed seed-macro mean gaps, but no reliable attention or
suppression mean gap.  This is not an equivalence test, a token-level
classification result, or a claim that every gold coordinate is positive and
every conflict coordinate negative.

\subsection{Audits, earlier links, and non-results}

For every intervention case, the instrumented baseline and its same-path no-op
have exactly zero margin and NLL drift.  The custom no-op is not numerically
identical to the native kernel: the maximum absolute pair-margin drift is
0.2838/0.2388/0.4638 at 8K/16K/24K, although top-one decisions agree for 8/8
seeds at every length.  We therefore identify local effects inside a
decision-preserving custom graph; we do not add the deltas to native logits.

The earlier links remain useful but have different evidential roles.  Exact
frequency reconstruction establishes phase-to-score equivalence.  Residual
replay accounts for the observed layerwise difference, and activation patching
shows sufficiency in one extended-position transition.  The population
singleton experiment closes the local score-to-Value/residual-to-margin step.
Together they form a coherent mechanism account, but they do not yet estimate
the fraction of an output change mediated by phase, denominator competition,
Value writes, and later Query drift separately.

Finally, the intervention is not a quality method.  Paired gold-NLL differences
between oracle evidence targets and matched randoms are $-0.02239$
[$-0.09654$, 0.03315], $-0.00569$ [$-0.04247$, 0.02227], and $-0.00325$
[$-0.04373$, 0.03686] at 8K/16K/24K; all intervals include zero.  We therefore
make no PPL, free-generation, or accuracy-improvement claim.  A separate NF4
pre-RoPE proposal diagnostic is reported in \cref{app:diagnostic-retrieval}
only as evidence that phase-suppressed candidates can sometimes be recovered.
