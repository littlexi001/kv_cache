\section{From RoPE phase to directed answer utility}
\label{sec:mechanism}

\subsection{First layer: fixed content, distance-dependent score}

RoPE groups the head dimension into $d/2$ two-dimensional pairs.  Let
$\omega_i$ denote the effective inverse frequency exposed by the active rotary
module; without frequency rescaling, $\omega_i=\theta^{-2i/d}$.  Let $\kappa$
be the fixed multiplicative rotary scale applied to each of Q and K.  For pair
$i$, write
\begin{equation}
  \preq_i=\begin{bmatrix}q_{i,x}\\q_{i,y}\end{bmatrix},\qquad
  \prek_i=\begin{bmatrix}k_{i,x}\\k_{i,y}\end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Because rotations form a group,
\begin{equation}
  (\kappa\Rope_t\preq)^{\top}(\kappa\Rope_j\prek)
  =\kappa^2\preq^{\top}\Rope_{j-t}\prek
  =\kappa^2\preq^{\top}\Rope_{-\Delta}\prek.
  \label{eq:relative-rope}
\end{equation}
Expanding one pair gives
\begin{align}
  s_i(\Delta)
  &=\kappa^2\!\left[A_i\cos(\Delta\omega_i)+B_i\sin(\Delta\omega_i)\right],
  \label{eq:pair-score}\\
  A_i&=q_{i,x}k_{i,x}+q_{i,y}k_{i,y},\qquad
  B_i=q_{i,x}k_{i,y}-q_{i,y}k_{i,x}.
\end{align}
Equivalently, with $\rho_i=\sqrt{A_i^2+B_i^2}$ and
$\psi_i=\operatorname{atan2}(B_i,A_i)$,
\begin{equation}
  s_i(\Delta)=\kappa^2\rho_i\cos(\Delta\omega_i-\psi_i).
  \label{eq:amplitude-phase}
\end{equation}
The content vectors determine amplitude and preferred phase; position supplies
the phase shift.  A semantically useful pair can therefore contribute
positively at one distance and negatively at another.  The full head score is
$s^R(\Delta)=d^{-1/2}\sum_i s_i(\Delta)$, a finite mixture of oscillatory terms
rather than a monotone distance penalty.

If $m_f$ filler tokens move the query while the key remains fixed, the exact
change for pair $i$ is
\begin{align}
  \delta s_i
  &=2\kappa^2\sin\!\left(\frac{m_f\omega_i}{2}\right)
  \left[
  -A_i\sin\!\left(\frac{(2\Delta+m_f)\omega_i}{2}\right)
  +B_i\cos\!\left(\frac{(2\Delta+m_f)\omega_i}{2}\right)
  \right],
  \label{eq:pair-delta}\\
  |\delta s_i|
  &\le 2\kappa^2\rho_i\left|\sin\!\left(\frac{m_f\omega_i}{2}\right)\right|.
  \label{eq:pair-bound}
\end{align}
High frequency increases local sensitivity, but only pairs with large content
amplitude $\rho_i$ matter.  Thus neither ``high frequency means local head''
nor ``longer always means worse'' follows from RoPE alone.

\paragraph{Exact first-layer check.}
In a seeded two-hop diagnostic, we load Qwen3-8B in NF4 with BF16 compute,
$d=128$, $\theta=10^6$, and YaRN factor 2.  We freeze the same first-layer
$\preq$ and $\prek$ and vary only their relative positions.  Every pre-RoPE
coordinate and norm is identical across four runs.  Reconstruction uses the
model's saved effective YaRN inverse frequencies and $\kappa=1.05359$ on each
rotated vector, not the unscaled base-frequency formula alone.
Mean post-RoPE gold QK over 32 query heads changes from $-5.588$ at distance
7,996 to $-33.586$ at 65,340.  Summing \cref{eq:pair-score} over all 64 pairs
reconstructs the explicit rotation with maximum absolute error 0.00198.  This
does not claim that a negative layer-0 mean determines the answer; it isolates
the first causal input: phase can change a fixed content score.

\subsection{Softmax amount is not directed evidence utility}

For a gold token $g$ and competitor $c$ in the same softmax,
\begin{equation}
  \frac{a_g}{a_c}=\exp(s_g-s_c).
  \label{eq:attention-ratio}
\end{equation}
Reducing the relative gold score by one multiplies its attention advantage by
$e^{-1}\approx0.368$.  More generally,
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\partial a_p}{\partial s_j}
  =a_p(\mathbf 1[p=j]-a_j).
  \label{eq:softmax-jacobian}
\end{equation}
Added filler can therefore hurt in two separable ways: relative position can
change $s_g$, while additional exponentiated scores enlarge the denominator.
Both affect how much evidence is read, but neither determines the direction of
its downstream effect.

Let the attention-weighted Value mean for head $h$ and its block contribution
through the output projection be
\begin{equation}
  \bar v_{l,h}=\sum_p a_{l,h,p}v_{l,g(h),p},
  \qquad
  o_{l,h}=W_{O,l}^{[h]}\bar v_{l,h},
  \label{eq:value-mean}
\end{equation}
Differentiating through softmax yields the exact local identity
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\partial o_{l,h}}{\partial s_{l,h,j}}
  =a_{l,h,j}W_{O,l}^{[h]}(v_{l,g(h),j}-\bar v_{l,h}).
  \label{eq:centered-value-derivative}
\end{equation}
Let $g_{l,h}=\nabla_{o_{l,h}}m$ denote the gradient of the final gold--conflict
margin through all subsequent computation.  The answer-directed score utility of
coordinate $(l,h,j)$ is
\begin{equation}
  U_{l,h,j}
  =\frac{\partial m}{\partial s_{l,h,j}}
  =a_{l,h,j}\,g_{l,h}^\top W_{O,l}^{[h]}
  (v_{l,g(h),j}-\bar v_{l,h}).
  \label{eq:directed-utility}
\end{equation}
This factorization separates \emph{amount} $a_{l,h,j}$, \emph{content direction}
$v_{l,g(h),j}-\bar v_{l,h}$, and \emph{answer direction} $g_{l,h}$.  A high-attention token
can have negative utility; a low-attention token can have positive utility.
For a small singleton intervention,
\begin{equation}
  m(s_{l,h,j}+\epsilon)-m(s_{l,h,j})
  =\epsilon U_{l,h,j}+O(\epsilon^2).
  \label{eq:first-order-margin}
\end{equation}
The derivative is an oracle diagnostic because computing $g_{l,h}$ uses the known
answer margin.  It explains a mechanism; it is not a deployable selector.

\subsection{Later layers: phase-triggered state divergence}

For each case $c\in\{S,T\}$, let $H_{l,c}$ be the complete token-state array
entering layer $l$, with positions $P_c$ and mask $M_c$, and let
$h_{l,c}=\pi_qH_{l,c}$ be only its final Query row.  Define the Query-row branch
outputs and exact real-valued update as
\begin{align}
  \mathcal A_{l,c}&=\pi_q\mathcal A_l(H_{l,c};P_c,M_c),&
  u_{l,c}&=h_{l,c}+\mathcal A_{l,c},\\
  \mathcal M_{l,c}&=\mathcal M_l(u_{l,c}),&
  h_{l+1,c}&=u_{l,c}+\mathcal M_{l,c}.
\end{align}
The two complete arrays may have different lengths; we subtract only their
Query-row quantities, writing $\delta x=x_T-x_S$.  This gives
\begin{equation}
  \delta h_{l+1}
  =\delta h_l+\delta\mathcal A_l+\delta\mathcal M_l.
  \label{eq:layer-difference}
\end{equation}
This is a Query-row vector identity: the three norms do not add because pairwise
inner products matter.  In BF16, inserting the two actual rounding operators replays
all 36 layers with zero recorded error; \cref{app:layerwise} gives the full
expression.

The score change in a later layer has three first-order sources,
\begin{equation}
  \delta s_{l,j}\approx\frac{\kappa^2}{\sqrt d}\left[
  \delta q_l^\top\Rope_{\Delta_j}k_{l,j}
  +q_l^\top\Rope_{\Delta_j}\delta k_{l,j}
  +q_l^\top\delta\Rope_{\Delta_j}k_{l,j}
  \right].
  \label{eq:three-score-sources}
\end{equation}
where both trajectories use the same rotary scale $\kappa$.
At layer 0, the final query embedding and hence $\delta q_0$ can be zero while
phase and the competing K/V set still alter attention.  From layer 1 onward,
\cref{eq:centered-value-derivative} makes projected query and key content
diverge as well.  The mechanism is therefore a changed nonlinear trajectory,
not repeated rotation of one fixed Q/K pair.

\paragraph{Exact replay and causal patching.}
For the separate, unquantized BF16 one-hop age diagnostic at
$143{,}424$-to-$143{,}488$ with YaRN factor 4 in \cref{fig:mechanism}, the query
input difference is zero.  Layer-0 attention and MLP differences have norms 0.542 and
1.293 and produce residual difference 1.466; the residual difference reaches
312.676 after layer 35.  The final RMSNorm and unembedding reconstruct the
target margin to $3.81\times10^{-6}$ and gold probability to
$3.73\times10^{-8}$.  Replay is descriptive, so we also patch the successful
residual state into the failed run.  Patching at layer 16 changes the margin
from $-2.75$ to $+0.625$; at layer 20 it restores $+1.00$.  This single
high-resolution case shows that intermediate state divergence is sufficient to
switch the decision.  The population intervention in \cref{sec:experiments}
tests the local score-to-margin link across lengths; it does not yet estimate a
complete mediated fraction for every arrow in \cref{fig:overview}, nor is it the
same prompt protocol as this trajectory case.
