\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Long-context failure and evaluation.}
LongBench, RULER, Lost in the Middle, and $\infty$Bench show that nominal
context length overstates reliable retrieval length and that evidence position,
task structure, and distractors matter~\citep{bai2024longbench,hsieh2024ruler,
liu2024lost,zhang2024inftybench}.  Our controlled dense sweeps target the
internal transition behind such failures rather than proposing another
aggregate benchmark.

\paragraph{RoPE extension and long-range geometry.}
Position interpolation, YaRN, LongRoPE, LongRoPE2, SelfExtend, and Resonance
RoPE modify frequencies or effective distances to extrapolate context windows
~\citep{chen2023positioninterpolation,peng2024yarn,ding2024longrope,
shang2025longrope2,jin2024selfextend,wang2024resonance}.  Hybrid or alternative
schemes such as LM-Infinite, RoPE-to-NoPE, and HoPE reduce the role of fine
position at long range~\citep{han2024lminfinite,yang2025rnope,chen2025hope}.
Recent analyses further identify long-distance dimension inefficiency,
attention-distribution changes, and position/token collisions under RoPE
~\citep{chiang2025dimension,zhong2025understanding,du2026rope}.  We complement
these works by reconstructing fixed-content phase effects and tracing how a
score perturbation acquires signed answer utility through Value and residual
computation.

\paragraph{Sparse and external retrieval.}
Longformer, BigBird, and H2O establish sparse or retained-token attention
patterns~\citep{beltagy2020longformer,zaheer2020bigbird,zhang2023h2o}.
LongHeads selects query-dependent remote chunks per head, while InfLLM combines
a local window with retrieved external memory~\citep{lu2024longheads,
xiao2024infllm}.  Most directly, SALS uses RoPE-free latent Q/K interactions to
select tokens before reconstructing selected Keys and applying native RoPE
sparse attention~\citep{mu2025sals}.  Consequently, pre-RoPE proposal followed
by native consumption is not our novelty; we use it only as a diagnostic of
recoverable phase-suppressed evidence.

\paragraph{Adaptive and semantic position.}
RePo learns context-dependent continuous positions from hidden states, and
AdaRoPE assigns different rotary behavior to attention heads
~\citep{li2026repo,wang2026adarope}.  These works delimit the design space for a
future semantic phase.  Our focus is different: we prove when a pairwise phase
repair can be represented by one reusable GQA cache and show why attention
amount or phase suppression alone cannot identify whether the associated Value
supports the answer.

The closest conceptual gap is therefore not another semantic retriever.  It is
the distinction among phase-sensitive score, softmax allocation, centered
Value direction, and answer-directed score utility, together with evidence
that the resulting difference propagates through a real model's residual
trajectory.
