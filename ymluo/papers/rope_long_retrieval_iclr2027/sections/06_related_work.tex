\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Long-context utilization and evaluation.}
Lost in the Middle demonstrates strong positional sensitivity even when
relevant information lies inside the input~\citep{liu2024lost}; LongBench and
RULER broaden evaluation to retrieval, reasoning, aggregation, and robustness
across lengths~\citep{bai2024longbench,hsieh2024ruler}.  These works establish
that nominal window size overstates usable context.  We complement task-level
measurement with an internal chain from a controlled position change to QK,
softmax mass, residual state, and output margin.

\paragraph{Extending RoPE.}
Position Interpolation compresses positions into a trained range
~\citep{chen2023positioninterpolation}; YaRN interpolates frequency bands and adjusts
attention temperature~\citep{peng2024yarn}; LongRoPE and LongRoPE2 search
dimension-dependent rescalings~\citep{ding2024longrope,shang2025longrope2}.
Resonance RoPE aligns wavelengths with observed positions
~\citep{wang2024resonance}, while analyses of RoPE extension relate frequency
mapping to attention uncertainty and retrieval~\citep{zhong2025understanding}.
These methods primarily improve extrapolation or effective window length.  Our
question is orthogonal: even after choosing an effective frequency map, should
fine-grained remote phase control semantic candidate admission?

\paragraph{Separating local position from global access.}
LM-Infinite caps effective relative distances~\citep{han2024lminfinite}, and
SelfExtend combines exact local attention with grouped remote positions
~\citep{jin2024selfextend}.  RNoPE alternates RoPE and NoPE layers
~\citep{yang2025rnope}, while HoPE preserves selected rotary components and
adds position-independent dimensions~\citep{chen2025hope}.  Empirical work
also finds that wide rotary angle ranges can leave attention-head dimensions
inefficient for long-distance retrieval~\citep{chiang2025dimension}.  Recent theory
shows that sufficiently long rotary contexts can exhibit position and token
aliasing, motivating a structural separation of locality and semantic
consistency~\citep{du2026rope}.  \method is closest in principle to this
local/global separation, but targets frozen pretrained models and separates
the \emph{candidate proposal} from exact post-RoPE \emph{consumption} inside
each head.  This conservative split enables a matched-budget causal test and
retains the model's learned positional scoring for every selected token.

\paragraph{Sparse attention and retrieval.}
Sparse attention methods reduce the set of keys processed at each query, using
fixed patterns, token importance, or approximate search
~\citep{beltagy2020longformer,zaheer2020bigbird,zhang2023h2o}.  Our current
prototype is not a systems competitor because its exact pre-RoPE proposal
still scans all keys.  Its contribution is a retrieval signal derived from the
RoPE failure mechanism.  Combining that signal with a practical low-bit or
approximate index is future work.
